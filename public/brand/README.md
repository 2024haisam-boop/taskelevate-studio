# Brand assets — DO NOT MODIFY THE ARTWORK

These are the real Taskelevate logo files supplied by the business (originals in `source/`, untouched;
the top-level files are transparent-margin crops only).

Approved usages (implemented in `src/components/layout/BrandLogo.tsx`):
1. **Horizontal lockup** (`variant="inline"`) — white wordmark + hairline + "STUDIOS" label (navigation).
2. **Icon only** (`variant="icon"`) — favicon, app icons, compact UI.
3. **Stacked** (`variant="stacked"`) — white wordmark + unchanged gradient icon with "STUDIOS" beneath the wordmark (footer, social image).

Rules: never redraw, recolour, distort or re-letter the icon or wordmark. Effects (glows, frames) may only be added
around the artwork with CSS/SVG. When a vector source arrives, replace the PNGs with identical-geometry exports.
